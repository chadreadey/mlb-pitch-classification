Ball or strike?

Place a pitch, pick the pitch type, set the count and the batter, and see the chance the umpire calls a taken pitch a strike. The model is a gradient-boosted tree ensemble trained on Statcast called pitches from 2022–24 and tested on 708K from 2025–26. It runs in your browser. Catcher's view.

A LightGBM model on location relative to the batter's own rulebook zone, the count, handedness, pitch type, and the pitch's speed, spin and movement. Each pitch type uses the median speed, spin and movement for that pitcher hand in 2022–23. Probabilities are isotonic-calibrated, and the model runs in the browser with ONNX Runtime Web.

Try a four-seamer against a sinker on the same corner, or 3-0 against 0-2. "Small" and "tall" use the 10th and 90th percentile zones from 2022–24.

Model and code: mlb-pitch-classification (scripts/gbm.py). Started as a 2023 class project with Carli Kelley and Garner Hasler. Rebuild and interactive version by Chad Readey.
